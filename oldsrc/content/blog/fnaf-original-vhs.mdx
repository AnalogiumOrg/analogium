FNAF VHS is a series originally created by Squimpus McGrimpus via YouTube. The series is made up of 2 seasons and is based on the **Five Nights At Freddy's** games (only covering the first 4 games), created by Scott Cawthon.

This version of the series, once praised and loved, is now infamous due to controversy regarding the creator, **gone for good** from the Internet for the last couple years. Fortunely the concept found some kind of redemption thanks to the [Battington Reimagination](/fnaf-vhs).

## SYNOPSIS
Old footage from Fazbear Entertainment is starting to arrise from the shadows, and is not your avarage work guides. These held the memories and story of the 8 components a giant curse: The Murderer, 5 Victims... and the **Hope of the Fallen**.

## INTERPRETATION

### SEASON 1 - GIVE GIFTS, GIVE LIFE
Our protagonist finds four tapes of the company Fazbear Entertainment, these tapes being material for the workers of his chain of pizzerias Freddy Fazbear's Pizza, and his famous animatronic mascots: Freddy, Bonnie, Chica and Foxy. The first tape shows a manual on how to clean the animatronics, using Freddy as an example. Step by step, it shows how to dismantle the animatronics for cleaning, until we get to the final part, where the torso is removed to reveal... **blood**.

> <ToggleImage src="climbinsidethetorso" />
> Freddy's insides.

The second tape tests the movements Bonnie can perform, in order to prevent any failure of her metallic joints. However, halfway through the tests Bonnie begins to act peculiarly, first remaining static, then seeking refuge in the shadows, until finally she attacks the camera and cuts the video. After a brief static, a mysterious gift appears in front of the camera, from the first victim and the one who plans to end it all once and for all: *The Puppet*.

The third tape prepares Chica to test her sound detection and see if everything works as it should... At this point it should come as no surprise that something weird ends up happening with Chica, but now it's something more "human"... She speaks **directly to the camera**.

The last tape shows a short prologue of Foxy the Pirate's show, where he invites the kids to show up in 3 minutes or there will be serious consequences, like sending Freddy and the rest of the crew to David Jones' Locker, or asking for a raise (I think that joke was more aimed at the parents)... until he presents the silhouette of 5 kids, of which one disappears. The video now repeats without music, and near the end the audio cuts out completely, being replaced with an alarm. Another of the children disappears, and the video repeats a third time, now with a synthetic voice demanding that we stop watching the tape. The video cuts in half and the last remaining children disappear, and from the shadows... ***"he" appears***.

It would seem that this is the end, but one last gift is given to our protagonist... *a fifth tape* that shows in depth everything that has happened in this haunted place, and how everything is going to end. A *familiar entity* starts talking to the protagonist of our story, about all the context that lead this place to the haunted state is now: **Michael Afton**.

The pizzeria, originally called Fredbear's Family Dinner, was a stupidly dangerous place thanks to the Spring-Lock technology they used for the animatronics, as it was completely unstable. The consecuences of this avoidable mistake would come soon enough, as Joseph Afton, his little brother, was bitten directly in the cranium by Fredbear jaws just for a dumb joke from Michael side. Since that day, William would go crazy and kill 5 childrens on the stablishment, being *Charlie Emily / The Puppet* the first one. She would since help the others by possessing the cosanimatronics, and plotting revenge against William and forcing him to die in a Spring-Lock failure. However they are still trapped, and now with Micheal's help, a happy ending looks possible, but that'll be after our **Horror Attraction**.

### SEASON 2 - I'M STILL HERE
We are far from the end: 7 new tapes enter the formula. The sixth tape is a training video for the night security guards, introducing the employees to the tools they will use, such as the camera system, the door controls, the danger posed by the animatronics, and a strange golden rabbit who wants to *talk to his boy*. At certain points in the film, certain distortions shows William in the shadows, trying to get closer to Michael, even giving him indications of where he is trapped.

The seventh film returns with the testing of the animatronics, now with the Toy models and their new facial recognition technology. Whoever is recording the test shows various photos to the robot, some containing innocent people and others containing people with criminal records; should the latter appear the animatronic will emit a subtle alarm. It sounds simple, and it is simple... until one last photo over-alarms the animatronic, who attacks the employee (who fortunately saved himself). But why, instead of alarming, did it attack? Toy Bonnie didn't recognise a criminal, it recognised ***THE CRIMINAL***.

> <ToggleImage src="criminal" />
> <StyleText id="youcant" content="C R I M I N A L"/>

The eighth tape, rather than work footage, is a security camera recording, capturing Chica and Bonnie leaving the stage. As the two meet between the tables, they start talking to each other about who might be in the office now, at which point Chica mentions that if it was "him" who is hiding, he should be sorry for what he did to her *good brother*; Bonnie starts to cry and is grateful that his sister doesn't know exactly what is going on. The two separate and the song **Lullaby of The Leaves** begins to play on stage. This particular film is the rawest of the whole collection, because it reminds us that, inside those killer pets, behind all the curse of the establishment... *there are only children waiting to go home*.

The ninth tape is a communiqué to the employees of Fredbear's Family Dinner, notifying them of the changes to be made to the pizzeria after the accident that killed Joseph, including the removal of Spring-Lock technology, and the replacement of Springbonnie and Fredbear with the foursome we all know best. Fazbear Entertainment's management had as a priority the public reaction and the constant questions that would come after these changes, however they already had a solution for that: **deny *everything***.

The tenth tape shows a cartoon based on the mascots of the pizzeria, and what they do after closing the place. This particular episode shows the gang getting ready for pizza for dinner, until they notice something strange in the dining room: there are 6 chairs and only 4 people. Freddy doesn't pay much attention to that and says it must be a "strange coincidence"; Chica, not entirely convinced, starts looking through boxes for something that shows the opposite of what Freddy said, until she finds a photograph of the band with two silhouettes of a rabbit and a bear. Strange sounds lead her to the kitchen, where she finds Golden Freddy who wants to talk to all of them: Joseph still doesn't trust his brother, he still hates him for everything that happened in '83; he can't save them, they would never go to heaven.

> <ToggleImage src="tomorrowisanotherday" />

The penultimate tape is a recording of William Afton himself, where he confesses to the murder of “The 5 Missing Childrens of Freddy's”, and delves into his motives and modus operandi. William was left with the anger of having lost his little Joseph to a son he always hated, and to free himself from all that torment he decided to drown himself with other children who were just like Michael in his eyes: complete brats. After confessing, William gives a final warning to whoever is listening to the tape: he checks the place where he hid the tape every day to see if it has moved, even if it is millimetres apart. So you'd better put the tape down Michael, **and start running**.

The final tape is a series of voice recordings of William after he died inside the Springbonnie suit, and how he was waiting for Michael from 1993 to 2008, from Freddy Fazbear's Pizza to Fazbear's Fright: **The Horror Attraction**. It's now or never, Michael is on the premises along with Puppet, the souls of the children, and his own father trapped on that old *spring trap*, ready to end this curse once and for all.
