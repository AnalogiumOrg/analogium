THE NIXONVERSE is a tale developed by M4NTICOR3 (Alex/Eve Casanas) via YouTube on 2022. The series is a Spin-Off of his main story, [**THE MONUMENT MYTHOS**](), and an expansion for one of his oldest videos, [**THE HOUSE IN THE OCEAN**]().
In 2023 it got an expansion called "**THE ABSOLUTE NIXONVERSE**".

## SYNOPSIS
The series take place after the events of the second season of MONUMENT MYTHOS, in a different universe with a fairly familiar form; the story connects three **containers for God's *GOODNESS*, *WILL* and *AUTHORITY***, who slowly become mad thanks to one simple thing: *our world is not kind, **our world will never be kind***.

## INTERPRETATION

### PROLOGUE - THE END OF ANOTHER WORLD
Before anything can happen, we need to adress one key element of our world, **The Nature of Alcatraz Matter**: if we put together a regular atom and an Alcatraz atom, the particles of the regular one will mutate to the properties and characteristics of the Alcatraz one, acting similarly to a parasite than anything else. This allegory makes more sense knowing that every Alcatraz atom has *its own mind, brain and neurons*. Now with knowladge captured, we can ***wake the Horned Serpent***.

### ACT I - THE CHILDREN OF GOD
Our story begins with a docummental created by Alice Avenue, talking about a hero who turned the fate of the Amercian soldiers that arrived to Normandy and accepted death as their destiny. They baptized him as **The D-Day Knight**, and prayed that at any time, at any place, he could save us. During D-Day, the Knight lost his helmet and when researchers found it in the battlefield, it had a lot of damage. Luckly for us, they managed to restore it to its full glory... however something looked odd, why a knight would have a whole craneum as his helmet? What kind of *horned creature* would ever have squeletic formation like that to begin with?

> <ToggleImage src="hornedhelmet" />
> D-Day Knight's Helmet

However, he's not the only hero of this land. There's another being borned taller than a man, yet stil decides to keep himself at the size of one: **The Last Son of Alcatraz.** He's naive and has a great desire to protect others, he's a better hero than the Knight would ever aspire to be, but even then he could conquer and hurt us all if he desires to. Finally the third container is showed to us, but not in our usual planet, this time is somewhere more special... like ***the Moon.***

The Apollo 11 odyssey captured in photos and recordings a woman living on the Moon, a queen waiting for her people to come back to her authority: **The Queen of the Lunarian.** Before Ed Dwight could step out of the lander and become the first man on the Moon, the Queen started a speech that she had prepared for a long time, the first ever recorded words on the Moon. Unfortunely a tiny blood stain covered the entire moment, as after commanding Ed to blast the Queen, John Glenn was *evicted from reality*.

### ACT II - THE HOUSE OF GOD
A queen for nobody living on the Moon, and the **Knight and Day** balance in between the Knight and the Son, it was just a matter of time for everything to go wrong... *way too wrong.* Going forward, in the middle of the Korean War, the Last Son ended up interviening in the name of America, even stopping the Knight for going "too far". Once his job was done, he returned to his fortress on Alaska, fully oblivious about the tiny present that followed him home. **American nuclear missiles**.

Why would the United States kidnap one of their heros? They have a big plan, but before it could take place they need something else: thanks to the research that Apollo 11 did on Lunarian anatomy, Apollo 12 was sended to the Moon with the objective to shoot the Queen and recover her eye. Once back on Earth, the big transformation of the Last Son took place, he was brainwashed and became a bastardized impersonator of our original savior, the one who will bring to our land a distorted vision of world peace: ***Jesus Christ***.

> <ToggleImage src="itwastheonlyway" />
> OUR FREEDOM ᐳ YOUR FREEDOM<br />OUR CHRIST ᐳ YOUR CHRIST<br />= WORLD PEACE

With our "Lord" created, the US sended the Son to theoreticlly redeem the rest of the world, starting on Vietnam (now called Bethlenam). They started spreading their vision and their power everywhere, like a virus, but at some point the Son regained his senses, he opened his eyes (well... his one eye) and he saw all the pain he created under the Lord mantra. He flew away into his hideout, now called Dark Alaska, and he hid inside an imitation of his childhood home: the Son built a house... [in the ocean]().

**The House in the Ocean is not an urban legend. IT IS A COVER STORY**

### ACT III - THE THRONE OF GOD
While all this havoc was happening on Earth, what was going on with the Queen? She was hiding inside an empty city underground, remembering all the events that brought her at this point, recalling her story.

She never knew how she got to the Moon, let alone how she was alive, but what she knew is that she was the Queen of... someone. She searched the entirety of the Moon and yet, no subjects were found. She hoped deep within herself that one day her kingdom would return, and by that time she wanted to get everything ready, she wanted her people to find confort and equality in her arms. She built a city below the surface, with houses, parks, air and her own throne, still no one in sight; until finally, after a long time a ship touched the surface of her Moon... but she was met with pain. The ship was from the US, and the astronauts aboard wanted **nothing but her eye**. Once they left and still wounded, the Queen crawled back to her lonely empire, and when she reached her throne something changed, her throne was vandalized and left with a message for the Queen:

> <StyleText id="lunarian" content="THE LONESOME QUEEN OF MOON ABOVESHALL BE STRONG FAIR AND KIND. SHE'LL RULE THE EARTH BOTH FEARED AND LOVED WITH KNIGHT AND KING BESIDE" />

The time of her lifetime has finally come, soon the **container of God's *ISOLATION*** would leave her empty world to rule a new, better one, whether they accepted her... or not. Out of the clouds of her sky, she crafted a ship and leaved the Moon forever. Her new kingdom was calling, but first a quick stop in Dark Alaska, right where the House in the Ocean resides. The Queen quickly notices that the powers and abilities of the Son were more similar to the King her prophecy told, so she invited him to join her kingdom and rule with her... it only took one little drastic action for the Son to reclaim his throne. He finally got to one final form, he's no longer either a man, a hero or the Lord, he was at last... **The Crescent King**.

### ACT IV - THE KNIGHT OF GOD

Queen and King started "lunarianizing" humans, resembeling the look of their king, with crescent heads and unstable anatomy. For those who still were humans, something worse was prepared for them, the Lunarians deleted their free choice to join the kingdom or rebel against it, they brainwashed them and deleted their culture, their language, their spirit. Even in the middle of the storm hope can rise... Alice Avenue was back and ready to pray for those who couldn't. Her plan was simple, broadcast the story of the Last Son, hoping for him to wake up and reverse himself.

The Last Son was a young man like you and me, with parents who were cutthroats and classmates who were worse. The boy was always alone when he went to school, and always in those moments driving towards his destiny, he doubted his role in the universe; he always thought that he had to help everyone around him, and that he had to win at any cost. One day in the midst of his thoughts, the young man was involved in a car crash; still strong enough to move, he got out of his car and approached the dying man with whom he had collided, a familiar face from another universe... **James Dean**.

"You won, little bastard... you won...", this were the last words of the man who in this universe, died. The Last Son was devastated and could not hold back his tears, and from the pain and exhaustion caused by the accident, he fainted. He woke up in a strange dream afterwards, where he found himself on a cliff with a vehicle driving at high speed towards him. He, to avoid the impact, jumped into the void, and began to believe more and more that he could fly, that he could beat death... he did it. The young man began to fly high, faster than an airplane. It was right here that he woke up and flew as far away as possible from the scene of the accident. He kept on believing, and kept on winning at any cost in order to save everyone. Always up in the sky, **it's a bird, it's a plane, it's The Last Son of Alcatraz**.

Alice not only prayed that the Crescent King would remember the hero he was before becoming a **container of God's *PAIN***, she also hoped that the Knight would return to save us all again. He listened to her words and executed a desperate plan to make the humanity of the Last Son wake up again: throw Alice off the top of the Chrysler Building hoping that he would rescue her... **that never happened**.

> <ToggleImage src="avenuefall" />
> Alice Avenue last moments of life

After the Knight's plan failed in every way, the Lunarians continued their plans. It's at this point that the Knight would compose a message apologizing for what happened to Alice, and that she was still alive... "**IN MY WORLD, STATUES HELD SOULS**".

Yet, something inside the Knight left him unsatisfied, something made him feel hollow and just angry at himself, at being a terrible American and dissapointing his whole country. To try to redeem himself he removed the head of the Alice Avenue memorial statue and wear it as one of his old helmets; even though he had only worn it for a day something in him changed, the only thing that could be seen was his eye, full of anger... he become a **container of God's *RAGE***.

His body was found right there, and his autopsy determined that his death was caused by his own ray-gun; next to him was a tape which would be referred to as "THE MOON GOD". This tape holds a conversation between Ed and someone special, the one behind the existance of the Son, the Knight and the Queen: The God of this universe... *Richard Nixon*.

In the present time, the prophecy is still missing one final being for the Lunarian kingdom to be whole: the Knight. Telepathically, the Queen tries to convince him to leave the land that broke him and join the kingdom as Prince. However, the Knight, amidst all the anger he has, still remember the words of Alice, that "at any time, at any place, the Knight could SAVE US", and unleashes a final movement that would restore the damages made by the Lunarians, he exploded his soul into pure Alcatraz matter, wich started to attack the Lunarian bodies, destroy the cloud ships and restore human culture: ALCATRAZ CHECKMATE.

However, there is still the King, who has finally become aware of all he has done, and breaks down in tears once again. Grief made him a monster and he just wants to dissapear from his own misery, but in an instant, he mutates again, becoming something [too dangerous](https://youtu.be/L7yjXlmDZ_A), exactly what Nixon feared he would become... **THE HORNED SERPENT LIVES.**

> <ToggleImage src="thehornedserpentlives" />

Nixon must act fast, he can't let it all end like his own world ended, so he decided to render everything as fiction, the same way our monsters became nothing but urban legends. That's how this whole world was fictionalized, and became a comic known as "The Nixonverse", with an endearing story made by a certain "Mister Manticore" and beautifully illustrated by... **Ed Dwight?**

Compared to Nixon, Ed Dwight was twice the God he was, Nixon was just one of many survivors who erased the past, to run from the present. Ed was everything Nixon wasn't, someone bold, kind and good. He never went to the Moon, let alone talked to a God, he is today in our world creating monuments... *Ed Dwight preserves the past*.
