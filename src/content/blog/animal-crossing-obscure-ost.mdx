Animal Crossing: Wild World [Obscure OST] is an ARG created by sepiatone via YouTube on 2023. The videos were unlisted and they can only be accessed by a hidden playlist.

## SYNOPSIS
A set of songs "unscraped songs" from Animal Crossing: Wild World, with no sight of ever existing or even mentions of it on the game files, are uploaded to YouTube. While at first it may seem like a case of lost media being found, it couldn't be farther from the truth, as a strange almost cursed version of the game hides between what should just be a set of **forgotten** tracks.
