ANGEL HARE is a mini-series created by the Mangan Sisters (**The East Patch Productions**) via YouTube. Since it beginning on Autumn 2022 it gained a big amount of popularity, as it does something different with this genre so well known for it scary scenarios: *It isn't scary.* The vibe it gives is a calm, nostalgic, like a constant daydream so safe and pacific, and yet sometimes it can get **unsettling** to say the least.

## SYNOPSIS
Jonah, a man who repressed the memories of his past, finds a collection of VHS tapes from the series of his childhood, 'Angel Hare'. Little did he knew that the simple act of getting them would throw him into a deep *rabbit-hole* of his own conciousness, a jorney between the verge of his senses to answer one simple question: **Where did my angel go?**

## INTERPRETATION

### SEASON 1: TAPES

> THIS SHOW WAS MY COMFORT,
> MY ESCAPE.
> BUT WHEN I PUT IN THE TAPE...
> **IT WASN'T THE SAME**

Jonah Whitman, our main testifier for this tale, is a man who repressed the memories of his childhood, leaving the past as a dense fog. One day, as if destiny decided it, he finds a very special VHS tape on a thrift store, containing all the episodes from a christian cartoon known as "Angel Hare". This spark was the cause of a wildfire deep withing Jonah's mind, unveiling the architecture of many forgotten days... and yet, **something was off.**

The episodes weren't as Jonah remembered them, the events that he got stuck on his memory didn't matched up with any of the one happening on the tape. It could be another case of [Mandela](/mandela-catalogue) Effect, but Jonah didn't thought the same, as he managed to recover old VCR recording he made whenever the show aired.

> #### VHS
> Angel Gabby helps Friend Francis to introduce to the audience and become friends, first introducing herself, asking them to do the same and later on asking to share something that they like, like a book or a toy. Gabby's always looking at the audience.

> #### VCR
> Angel Gabriel helps Friend Francis to introduce to their new friend **Jonah**, first introducing herself, letting him introduce himself and later on asking him to share something special to him, the book [Babe: The Gallant Pig](). Gabby moves her eyes before talking about Jonah's book, almost like she was **looking him**.

The intrigue and mystery took over Jonah, as he started to make research on the origins of the series. The start launched him directly to a concrete wall, as the original publisher "**[Wreath of Life](https://www.wreathoflife.org/)**" seem to have zero footprints he could research. The differences between VHS and VCR get bigger and more notorious; Gabby start talking directly to Jonah about being stronger during dark times, even breaking the whole plot point of one episode. Instead of following Francis she flies to the clouds to show Jonah *how to be **Strong***.
