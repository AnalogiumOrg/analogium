My dear friend, always surrounded by cogs and wires. The mastermind behind all the machines and protocols inside the complex. Everytime I get a new idea, an idea that has to do with mechanical tools or specific algorithms, he can work around it. The first real team member that I got in the project, if we, by one curse or another, lose him, the consequences would be **catastrophic**. The ideas would be limited, the system would need to work on a slower pace, and in the case of them breaking, there would be no easy way-out of that.
