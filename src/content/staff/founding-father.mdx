I can describe myself as a visionary, a man in eternal slumber, trapped on a lucid fever dream. When I created ANALOGIUM, I was at first alone, stranded on an island and forced to find a way forward, trying to keep everything in place with only my hands, sometimes succeeding, and other times... well you can figure that one out. Every time I went back to my office, thinking on more and more ideas, more stories I could fit in, and how could I expand the team since these cold walls were acting more like a private asylum. Then it clicked, who was I before chaining my soul to this new mantra? A nobody. A troubled child surrounded by cutthroats. A plague. Could it be there another instance of my own phenomenon?

I settled on the task of giving those rejects the helping hand they needed, the same hand I crafted for myself so long ago... oh Lord how disappointed I am at you, I expected a harder challenge. My team was lost, scared, vulnerable, "foolish" if one of you normal-craniums want to put it like that. It took a little bit of time to get the used to this cold compelx, but it was all worth it. The first one I could get inside the team was my dearest and closest friend: THE MACHINERY. If I ever get another idea, he is always there to help me make it a reality. I can't deny that there were some old members, they left for reasons beyond my control, some ment a big loose to our team, a whole hard to fill, and some others... _are better gone_.

This place, this society I managed to craft, the same minded fellas I got the chance of discovering (for the better or for the worse), is the biggest project I had. Me and my team will continue to archive all these amazing stories, even if the media dies tomorrow, or the normal-craniums find something new to be scared of. **Fear is forever.**
