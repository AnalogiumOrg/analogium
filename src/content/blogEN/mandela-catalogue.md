The Mandela Catalogue is a story created by Alex Kister via YouTube. The series has one full season (dubbed Act I) and a second in development. It is considered to be the series that popularised Analogue Horror.

## SYNOPSIS

Mandela County is under constant terror and mystery, not from an alien invasion or a zombie virus, but from <i>"demons"</i> that mimic the local villagers. These monsters have been on our planet since the beginning of time, I think some people place it as the [<font style="color: rgb(255, 255, 255)">Genesis](https://quod.lib.umich.edu/cgi/k/kjv/kjv-idx?type=DIV1&byte=1477).
