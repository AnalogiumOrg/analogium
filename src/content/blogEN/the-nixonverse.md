THE NIXONVERSE is a tale developed by MISTER MANTICORE (Alex Kansas) via YouTube. The series is a Spin-Off of his main story,  [THE MONUMENT MYTHOS](../the-monument-mythos).

## SYNOPSIS

The series take place after the events of the second season of MONUMENT MYTHOS, in a different universe with a familiar form; the story connects four entities very similar and powerful: the Son, the Knight, the Queen and... <i>God</i>.

With these entities, there are some eyes that glow more than others, those being from Alice Avenue, Ed Dwight, and the egocentric self of the United States, always obsessed with power.

## INTERPRETATION

<b><i>Faster than an airplane! More powerful than a locomotive! Impervious to bullets! Up in the sky, look! It's a bird! It's a plane! It's - -</i></b>

Our story begins with The Last Son, a real superhero who is always there to save us all, and yet some people are in constant fear that he will rebel against us and conquer us.

Actually he is not our only hero, there is another one baptised The D-Day Knight, the one who saved the US Army on the battlefield that day in 1944. He gave us all hope and faith from his intervention in Normandy, and no one more than Alice Avenue had great respect and affection for him. That's the beginning of our planet, but what if we go up? Somewhere like... <i>the Moon</i>.

The first mission to land humans on the Moon was not as normal as you might think, because Ed Dwight (the first man on the Moon) and his team found the Queen of the Lunarians, and witnessed the first recorded words on our natural satellite. Unfortunately, a small bloodstain covered the moment, because after ordering Ed to shoot her, John Glenn was <i>evicted from reality</i>.
