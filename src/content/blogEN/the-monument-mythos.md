The Monument Mythos is a tale developed by MISTER MANTICORE (Alex Casanas) via YouTube. The series have 2 full seasons, a third one in development and a Spin-Off known as [THE NIXONVERSE](../the-nixonverse).

## SYNOPSIS

The series chronicles the strange happenings that America doesn't usually talk about, or do you think an entire government would chatter about a monster inside the Statue of Liberty like it's nothing?

Some similar things happen all over the country (and possibly somewhere in Egypt), like an entire island that actively sprawls, a parallel dimension, an 'angel' inside the Air-Force One and a crystal as sharp as it is mysterious.
